Our Mission: Knowledge is Free

SkillSwap was born at SIES Graduate School of Technology with a simple idea: everyone is a master of something and a student of everything else. We've built a platform where your expertise is the only currency you need.

Why we started SkillSwap

In a world of expensive courses and gatekept knowledge, we believe in peer-to-peer growth. Whether you're a second-year student teaching Data Structures or a fitness enthusiast sharing workout routines, you're helping build a smarter community.

  • ✓ Zero cost, 100% community-driven learning.
  • ✓ Build your portfolio through teaching others.
