Frequently Asked Questions?

Everything you need to know about the SkillSwap ecosystem.

How does the skill swapping process work?
Simply find a partner with the skill you want, offer a skill you excel in, and once both agree, you can start your exchange sessions through our built-in video or chat tools.
Is SkillSwap free to use for students?
Our Core plan is 100% free. You earn "Swap Credits" by teaching others, which you can then spend to learn new skills from anyone in the community.
How do I know the mentor is qualified?
Every user has a profile showing their "Impact Badge" and ratings from previous swaps. We encourage reviewing a partner's portfolio before committing to a session.
Can I swap non-technical skills like Design?
Absolutely! SkillSwap covers everything from MERN stack development to Canva design, photography, and even soft skills like public speaking.
What happens if a swap session doesn't go as planned
We prioritize community trust. If a session is cancelled or the quality isn't up to mark, you can report the issue to our support team. We also use a rating and review system so you can see a mentor's track record before committing your credits.
