What We Offer

Empowering Your Learning Journey

SkillSwap isn't just a platform; it's a complete ecosystem designed to make teaching, learning, and collaborating seamless, highly verifiable, and entirely cash-free.

🤝

1-on-1 Skill Swapping

Exchange hours of expertise directly with peers. Teach something you know perfectly, and in return, get guided personal mentorship in a completely new domain, free of monetary barriers.

🤖

AI Authentication

Every profile is strongly verified. Our AI scanning framework analyzes GitHub repositories, detects forged certificates natively, and ensures instructors actually hold the skills they claim to teach.

✨

AI Study Tools

Powered by cutting-edge LLMs like Groq, instantly generate comprehensive study notes, interactive mind maps, flashcards, and quizzes customized to any topic within seconds directly on the platform.

Credit Economy

Operate seamlessly without cash. Spend your initial signup credits to take courses from experts. Host classes to earn credits back dynamically based on your verified skill levels and difficulty multipliers.

📊

Sleek Dashboard

Organize your ecosystem. Manage pending student requests, track your active sessions with interactive countdowns, and build learning tracks with your trusted peer network seamlessly.

📅

Integrated Scheduling

No more back-and-forth emails. Set your availability, select meeting types, automatically sync correct timezones across global borders, and coordinate swap sessions directly within our intuitive interface.
